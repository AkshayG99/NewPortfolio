### The Problem

Imagine you are a bank employee with a long line of frustrated customers in front of you. To resolve a single issue, you have to dig through thousands of old support tickets, parse confusing internal documentation, and manually fill out countless, complex regulatory forms. Every minute spent searching for data is a minute a customer spends waiting. The internal systems are vast, disconnected, and entirely devoid of intelligent search.

### Overview & Impact

During my time at RBC, I spearheaded the development of an intelligent NLP chatbot designed to answer queries and auto-fill forms. This initiative **boosted internal system efficiency by 70%** and is projected to save the organization **210,000 hours and $3.5M annually**. For my contributions, I received a Return Offer.

### Technical Implementation

The project required a full-stack approach, blending machine learning with robust enterprise infrastructure:
- **NLP & Machine Learning:** I fine-tuned a transformer-based embedding model (BERT) to significantly enhance the chatbot's contextual understanding and response accuracy.
- **Full-Stack Development:** I built the user interface and backend services using **React** and **Flask**. 
- **Database Automation:** Working with **IBM DB2**, I created dynamic SQL queries to automate the secure purging of sensitive data, successfully processing and purging over 100,000 entries.
- **CI/CD & DevOps:** I established robust Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) pipelines using **Jenkins** to enable automated testing and seamless deployment.

### Challenges

Balancing the strict data privacy requirements of a major financial institution with the need to train a robust NLP model was a significant hurdle. Ensuring that the IBM DB2 automated purging scripts ran flawlessly without accidentally deleting active transaction data required extensive, rigorous testing in the Jenkins CI/CD pipeline.

### Next Steps

The next phase of the project involves expanding the chatbot's capabilities to handle multi-turn, complex conversational flows regarding account discrepancies, and integrating voice-to-text functionality.
